Current Split & Routine

Push Day Chest • Shoulders • Triceps

Incline dumbbell press, flat bench press, overhead dumbbell press, lateral raises, tricep rope pushdowns. Focus on mind-muscle connection and controlled eccentrics.

Pull Day Back • Biceps • Rear Delts

Lat pulldowns, seated cable rows, dumbbell single-arm rows, face pulls, barbell/incline curls. Builds postural back strength essential for upright running posture.

Legs Day Quads • Hamstrings • Calves

Squats / leg press, Romanian deadlifts (RDLs), leg curls, walking lunges, standing calf raises. Heavy emphasis on knee stability and glute activation for marathon endurance.

Upper Body Day Compound Volume

High-volume accessory work blending chest, back, and arm movements for maximum metabolic burn and upper body definition.
