Outdoor Run Days (1-2/week)

  • Easy recovery runs (8:00-8:30 pace, 5-6 km)
  • Weekend long runs when weather is good (replace Saturday treadmill)
  • Practice 3:2 breathing rhythm outdoors

Rules to Follow

  1. Breathing: 3:2 pattern (inhale 3 steps, exhale 2 steps) at ALL speeds above 8.0 km/h
  2. No food: Nothing 2 hours before treadmill session
  3. Water: Small sips only, no big gulps. 200ml 30 min before, then 2-3 sips during recovery phases
  4. Incline: Always 1.0-1.5% on treadmill
  5. If stitch comes: Drop speed by 2 km/h for 2 min, do deep exhales, then resume
  6. Core: Never skip core days. This is what fixes the stitch permanently.

After 4 Weeks (What's Next)

If you complete Week 4 Day 3 successfully:

  • Increase to 9.0-9.5 km/h for tempo runs
  • Add hill intervals (incline 3-5% at 7.5 km/h)
  • Target: 5 km in under 30 min (6:00 pace = 10.0 km/h)
